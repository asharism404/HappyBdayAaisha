My dearest Aaisha, my chhotu,

Happy birthday, my love. Today the world got the girl who somehow became my favourite person, my calm, and my happiest thought.

I wish I could be standing next to you right now with a cake, a terrible singing voice and the biggest hug. Instead I’m sending you all of this, because that’s what we do: we make the distance smaller with every call, every message, every little moment we share.

Yes, we’re far apart right now, and some days it feels heavy. But you make the waiting worth it. Every time I talk to you, the miles disappear for a while.

And maybe one day, I truly believe it, you and I will be in the same place. No screens, no goodbyes at the end of the call. On that day this distance will just be a story we laugh about together.

Until then, I’m yours, wherever you are. Thank you for being you, chhotu. I love you more than any birthday wish can hold.

Forever yours,
